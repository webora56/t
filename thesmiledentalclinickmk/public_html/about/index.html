Our clinic

Patient-focused dental care

The Smile Dental Clinic is at Ghala Mandi, near Allied Bank, Kāmoke. We offer a range of dental services in a clean, comfortable environment and see patients by appointment.

Our approach is simple: listen to your concern, examine carefully, explain your options and treat with care. Our services include root canal treatment, braces and aligners, scaling and polishing, extractions, fillings, crowns and bridges, dentures, implants and retainers.
